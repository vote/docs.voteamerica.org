# Microsite and easy links

If you don't wish to embed our tools on your own website, VoteAmerica can host a custom-labeled microsite for you.
The microsite is a standalone website at `<your-requested-subdomain>.voteamerica.org` that includes our 
full suite of voter tools and state-by-state voting information, branded with your logo, colors, and messaging.
You can see an example of a microsite at [demomicrosite.voteamerica.org](https://demomicrosite.voteamerica.org/).

## Getting started

You'll need a free VoteAmerica Plus account at [secure.voteamericaplus.org/signup/](https://secure.voteamericaplus.org/signup/).

Then, visit [secure.voteamericaplus.org/microsite/](https://secure.voteamericaplus.org/microsite/). 
If there's already a microsite associated with your account, you'll find the details there. If not, you'll be able
to request a site, and it will be ready to use within a few minutes. 

## Easy links

Once your microsite is ready, you'll be able to access a set of easy links — one for each voter tool — 
that you can quickly grab and share in emails, social media posts, and other communications.
Find your links at [secure.voteamericaplus.org/microsite/](https://secure.voteamericaplus.org/microsite/).

## Customization 

Visit [secure.voteamerica.org/customization/](https://secure.voteamerica.org/customization/) 
to upload your logo and favicon, select your colors, and add custom messaging to your microsite.
